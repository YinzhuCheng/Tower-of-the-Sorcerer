# Story Clarity Audit · First-Read Pass · 2026-09-21

## Executive diagnosis

The shipped story is internally coherent, but its **explanation order is inverted for a first-time reader**.

The later acts, especially F21–F30, already contain enough concrete examples and causal explanation. The main readability problem is concentrated in:

1. the opening;
2. the first six floors;
3. the transition from F10 to F11;
4. the transition from F20 to F21.

A new player currently meets terms such as “回执 / 登记网 / 七段咏唱 / 结案 / 三席 / 主权权限” before the story has fully established:

- what Greyport is;
- what the Tower did during the evacuation;
- who Liyue was before the game began;
- what Shawu is;
- what Noctia was responsible for;
- why the seven guardians are both victims and opponents;
- why evidence collection matters as much as combat.

The result is not a contradiction problem. It is a **sequencing and context problem**.

## First-read acceptance standard

A first-time player should be able to answer the following after one continuous GAL-only playthrough:

1. What happened to Greyport three years ago?
2. What was the Tower responsible for?
3. Why did the emergency never end?
4. Who is Liyue and what was taken from her?
5. Who is Shawu and why can she guide Liyue?
6. Who is Noctia and why did she seal the Tower?
7. What are the seven cores?
8. Why do the guardians attack even when they recognize Liyue?
9. What evidence is being collected on each floor?
10. What caused the order to survive for three years?
11. Why is simply shutting down or destroying the Tower unsafe?
12. What exactly changes in Acts II and III?
13. What does the final archival solution do differently?
14. What emotional responsibility do Liyue, Noctia, and the Sovereign each carry?

## Character first-appearance audit

### 绫星·璃

Current issue: the story explains what happened to her power, but does not establish her ordinary identity early enough.

Required opening information:

- she took part in the Greyport evacuation;
- her seven-part escort chant coordinated multiple Tower functions;
- Noctia used those seven parts to keep the emergency network alive;
- Liyue returns to recover them, free the guardians, and reconstruct the truth rather than simply defeat a final boss.

### 残响精灵·纱雾

Current issue: “残响精灵” is presented as a title, but a first-time player is not told what that means.

Required opening information:

- Shawu is a magical echo / guide formed from surviving Tower communication traces;
- she can read low-level residual records and routes;
- she is not omniscient and cannot read sealed higher-authority records;
- this is why she can guide Liyue but cannot simply tell her the answer.

### 无声女王·诺克缇娅

Current issue: her protective intent appears, but only after the reader has already been asked to treat her as the final antagonist.

Required framing:

- she was responsible for the Tower’s emergency response;
- sealing the Tower during the storm was initially protective;
- she believed stopping the system would erase unresolved names;
- she is morally responsible for continuing the system after the cost became visible;
- she is not the sole technical cause of the three-year loop.

### Guardians

Each guardian should be introduced through:

1. their original civic function;
2. the human group they served;
3. the specific record/evidence they preserve;
4. how the core forces their body to continue an obsolete command.

## Terminology remediation

| Term | Plain-language first explanation |
|---|---|
| 回执 | A signed reply proving a ship / transfer actually arrived, not merely departed |
| 名簿 | The person-by-person manifest that says who was assigned to which evacuation route |

| 归档 | Preserve the original records while ending the command that actively drives guards/resources |
| 三席确认 | Three separate departments independently confirming the same emergency change |
| 总印 | A combined system mark claiming those separate confirmations agree |
| 无限延长 | Removing the original three-day stop condition from an emergency order |
| 起源核心 | The higher-level system that validates and executes authority across the Tower |
| 虚空先驱 | An automated interceptor created by the old order; not a secret human mastermind |
| 撤销签名 | The original authorized signer explicitly recording that a prior order is ended |
| 勘误核心 | A cleanup mechanism that resolves conflicting records, but under the old rules does so destructively |

## Style direction

Expansion is only valid if it adds at least one of:

- context;
- motive;
- causal explanation;
- sensory/action detail;
- relationship information;
- stakes;
- terminology clarification;
- scene transition.

Do not expand with interchangeable poetic filler.

The target is **readable, concrete Japanese-style GAL prose in Chinese**, not short UI copy and not an essay.
