# Fantasy Rewrite Narrative Lock — 2026-09-21

## Core premise

Three years ago, Gray Harbor invoked the ancient **Seven-Lamp Vigil** during a catastrophic evacuation.

The oath was simple: **until the last person has returned, the seven lamps must not go dark**.

The final evacuation vessel, Northstar Seven, actually reached the north shore. Its homecoming bell and captain's message never reached the tower. Fearing that silence meant death rather than safety, the Arcane Sovereign carved a forbidden **Vigil-Extension Seal**, changing a temporary vigil into an oath with no practical end.

Noctia, who never heard the final homecoming bell, kept the tower sealed. Liyue's seven-part chant was split among seven guardians so the vigil could continue. The tower then spent three years repeating one unfinished night.

The tragedy is magical rather than computational: **magic obeys the words of an oath, but does not understand the human intention behind them.**

## Act I — F1–F10: The Seven Lost Chants

Goal: Recover Liyue's seven chants and learn that the tower is trapped by an ancient vigil oath.

Key beats:
- Milu guards the first gate because the Moonshadow lamp still treats the night as ongoing.
- Feiye preserves the names of evacuees as living leaves.
- Lanyin holds the broken echo of Northstar Seven's final song.
- Serena reveals that the old sword oath originally contained an ending condition tied to the homecoming bell.
- Yanli proves the vigil fire is being forcibly reignited by a higher seal.
- Lumi finds a third path: names may remain even after the vigil ends.
- Yayu traces a hidden shadow-line to a seal above the throne.
- Vera opens the Silent Gate once all seven chants can answer one another.
- Seff reveals the author of the Vigil-Extension Seal: the Arcane Sovereign.
- Noctia leaves the throne and joins the ascent to find the missing bell.

## Act II — F11–F20: The Bell That Arrived Three Years Late

Goal: Recover the lost homecoming sound and perform the ending rite the tower never completed.

Key beats:
- F11 shows the missing fourth panel of the vigil rite: the ending rite itself was removed.
- F12 shows medicine blooming forever for people who are no longer there.
- F13 shows the forge being forced to relight by the Vigil-Extension Seal.
- F14 retrieves three independent memory crystals from the old oath-keepers.
- F15 uses physical keepsakes and Koko's delivery slip to anchor the timeline.
- F16 lets living witnesses preserve only what each actually saw or heard.
- F17 separates three seemingly identical “I agree” echoes and reveals they answered three different temporary requests.
- F18 follows the intercepted homecoming sound through the Clear-Sky Canal.
- F19 turns the old death ledger into the **Hall of Ten Thousand Lamps**, where returned, dead, and still-unknown people can coexist without being forced into one answer.
- F20 confronts the Arcane Sovereign, recovers the seven ley-lines, and finally plays the Northstar Seven bell through the Origin Wellspring.
- The Seven Lamps go dark without erasing a single name.

## Act III — F21–F30: The Road of Undelivered Letters

Goal: After ending the vigil, carry the words trapped inside the tower back to the living world.

Three optional late-game paths preserve the existing mechanical choice structure:
- **Moonshadow Escort Path** — defense / fewer counterattacks.
- **Starmirror Discernment Path** — offensive debuff / weaker finale enemies.
- **Crimson-Flame Relay Path** — MP capacity / refill timing.

Key beats:
- F21 releases letters, name-tags, tickets, farewells and keepsakes trapped by the old vigil.
- F22 restores safe passage for fragile letters.
- F23 uses the Starmirror to distinguish conflicting traces without “deleting” inconvenient evidence.
- F24 relights distant watchfires one by one so messages actually reach the outside world.
- F25 restores the torn final line of the ancient oath: the vigil may end while names remain.
- F26 gives Koko an ordinary future again: a shop that can finally close for the night.
- F27 converts the three late-game builds into three magical battlefield blessings.
- F28 turns the existing archive-storm scene into a literal storm of undelivered letters.
- F29 allows “unknown” to exist in the final name-book instead of forcing every story into a neat ending.
- F30 turns the Ember Lighthouse into the place that finally sends the tower's trapped words outward.

## Writing rules

Player-facing narrative should avoid administrative/computing vocabulary unless a rare UI explanation absolutely requires it.

Strongly avoid in spoken dialogue:
- 权限 / 校验 / 验证 / 授权
- 协议 / 程序 / 系统 / 节点
- 索引 / 写入口 / 缓存 / 数据 / 流程
- 结案 / 状态字段 / 版本

Preferred fantasy vocabulary:
- 誓 / 古誓 / 守夜 / 续夜之印
- 七灯 / 灵脉 / 咏唱 / 星镜 / 水镜
- 归航钟 / 船长信符 / 记忆晶
- 名灯 / 古卷 / 封蜡 / 灯塔 / 哨火

Also reduce argumentative prose patterns such as:
- “不是 A，而是 B”
- “只能证明……不能证明……”
- “真正的问题是……”
- “这不意味着……”

Prefer concrete magical action, character memory, objects, sound, light, weather, bodily reaction, and consequences.

## Asset policy

The rewrite treats existing character designs, portraits, maps and strong CGs as visual canon whenever possible. Rewriting should bend around accepted art before requesting replacement art. Only images whose visual content itself depends on modern UI / software-like diagrams should be replaced.
