# Character Voice Guide · 2026-09-21

This guide locks the major character voices for future GAL revisions. It is a prose constraint, not new canon.

## 绫星·璃 / Liyue

**Function in dialogue:** decide, separate causes, commit to the next action.

**Cadence**
- direct;
- medium-short sentences;
- prefers “先……再……” and concrete next steps;
- when summarizing evidence, she should compress rather than lecture.

**Use**
- “先把顺序钉死。”
- “下一步去……”
- “这件事和那件事要分开。”

**Avoid**
- long neutral explanations that could be spoken by Shawu;
- sounding like a design document;
- repeating every piece of evidence when the scene already established it.

## 残响精灵·纱雾 / Shawu

**Function in dialogue:** supply context, define evidence boundaries, warn against premature conclusions.

**Cadence**
- calm;
- precise;
- often distinguishes “what we know” from “what we still cannot prove”;
- gentle correction rather than command.

**Use**
- “先别急着下结论。”
- “能确定的是……”
- “这是一条线索，不是判决。”

**Avoid**
- omniscient exposition;
- announcing conclusions before the evidence is available;
- sounding emotionally identical to Liyue.

## 无声女王·诺克缇娅 / Noctia

**Function in dialogue:** express fear of loss, responsibility for coercion, and gradual willingness to stop controlling the outcome.

**Cadence**
- restrained;
- personal;
- concrete references to names, alarms, waiting, and choices she refused to make;
- rarely speaks in abstract system language unless recalling what she was shown.

**Use**
- “我不敢按下去。”
- “我告诉自己再等一晚。”
- “这一步该由我亲自做。”

**Avoid**
- pure victim framing;
- generic villain speeches;
- dry policy explanation.

## 龙姬·焰璃 / Yanli

**Function in dialogue:** make consequences physical.

**Cadence**
- speaks through heat, fuel, pipes, weight, burn time, and exhaustion;
- blunt;
- practical.

**Use**
- “这是一炉火，不是永远烧不尽。”
- “再没人替我们添柴。”
- “先把温度压下来。”

**Avoid**
- extended bureaucratic vocabulary;
- delicate abstract metaphors.

## 深蓝歌姬·澜音 / Lanyin

**Function in dialogue:** establish timing, authenticity, and evidence through sound.

**Cadence**
- slower;
- auditory;
- refers to echoes, rhythm, pauses, bells, voices and whether several sounds line up.

**Use**
- “时间晚，不等于声音真。”
- “船号、港钟和回声能对上。”
- “先听完整。”

**Avoid**
- sounding like a spreadsheet analyst;
- aggressive short commands better suited to Yayu.

## 影织姬·鸦羽 / Yayu

**Function in dialogue:** expose hidden paths, authority routes and operational traps.

**Cadence**
- short;
- sharp;
- minimal emotional ornament;
- uses spatial/path language: line, fork, source, trace, cut, hide.

**Use**
- “找到了。”
- “别踩那条线。”
- “先击败谁，就是选择谁。”

**Avoid**
- multi-paragraph tutorials;
- warm reassurance;
- repeating Shawu's full explanation.

## 奥术主权者 / Arcane Sovereign

**Function in dialogue:** own the decision, explain the original reasoning without asking for absolution, accept traceable responsibility.

**Cadence**
- sober;
- first person;
- repeatedly names what **he** signed, designed, failed to anticipate, and must answer for.

**Use**
- “这条规矩是我留下的。”
- “签下它的是我，撤销它的也该是我。”
- “不要把代价从记录里删掉。”

**Avoid**
- “I was forced” framing;
- speeches that transfer responsibility to the core;
- emotional self-pity.

## 猫卫长·米露 / Milu

**Function in dialogue:** guard people and objects physically; translate abstract responsibility into “can I carry/protect/deliver this?”

**Cadence**
- personal and protective;
- refers to hands, claws, doors, children, packages, keeping something from being dropped.

**Avoid**
- optimization jargon.

## 狐祝·绯叶 / Feiye

**Function in dialogue:** insist that individual names and records remain attached to real people.

**Cadence**
- careful;
- ritual/order vocabulary;
- sensitive to erasure, misfiling and treating people as totals.

## 剑圣·塞蕾娜 / Serena

**Function in dialogue:** define rules, boundaries, and discipline.

**Cadence**
- spare;
- martial;
- responsibility is expressed as “what the rule was for” and whether the current action still serves that purpose.

## 天穹魔女·露米 / Lumi

**Function in dialogue:** compare possibilities and expose contradictions.

**Cadence**
- analytical;
- explicit ordering (“first / second” is acceptable);
- speaks in modeled consequences rather than moral judgments.

## Global voice rules

1. If a line could be moved to another major character unchanged, rewrite it.
2. Do not let a speaking character narrate their own action in third person. Use narration for that.
3. Mechanics may be truthful without being spoken as UI copy.
4. Let Liyue decide, Shawu qualify, Yayu trace, Lanyin listen, Yanli feel the physical cost, Noctia confess/choose, and the Sovereign own the authored rule.
5. Preserve first-read clarity even when shortening a line for voice.
