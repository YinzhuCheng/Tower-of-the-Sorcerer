## 生图硬约束

Adult fox shrine officiant, 168 cm, long chestnut-red hair to hips, green eyes, two fox ears and exactly THREE separate red fox tails with white tips. White cross-collar wide-sleeved ceremonial top, crimson collar and cuff borders, ankle-length deep-green skirt, plain deep-green waist sash. ONE simple red leaf-shaped hair clip at HER RIGHT temple. Flat sandals. No flower clusters, bells, chains, flame embroidery or extra tails. All three tails originate at lower back, separated for counting in rear view.

One folding fan with dark wood ribs, plain ivory paper and ONE red disk, no written characters. Exactly THREE rectangular ivory paper seals carried in a plain green belt pocket; each marked by one simple red vertical stroke only, no decorative script. Fan stored at HER LEFT waist.
