状态：新简化设计；待图文交叉验收。此文件只适用于GAL，魔塔原图不受影响。

## 简化设定

火红长发、琥珀眼，两只黑角有金尖；一条黑红龙尾。黑色铠裙、红色内裙、窄金边；背后短披肩裁成两片翼形，不画实体翅膀。护甲使用大块整片。唯一武器为红缨长枪，取消龙头肩甲与多余角冠。

设计身高：175 cm（本轮新增比例基准，不是原剧本事实）。

## 生图硬约束

Adult dragon knight woman, 175 cm, long flame-red hair, amber eyes, exactly TWO short black swept-back horns with gold tips, exactly ONE black-red dragon tail. Black armored dress with broad red inset skirt and narrow muted-gold trim, simple black boots and gauntlets, two smooth shoulder plates. Short red mantle cut into TWO wing-shaped cloth panels, NOT anatomical wings. No crown, fur trim, dragon-head pauldrons, extra horns, scales engraved into every surface or multiple gemstones.

## 武器与物件

One slim spear, overall length 190 cm, straight black shaft, simple gold-edged red leaf-shaped spearhead and ONE short crimson tassel beneath blade. Tail has plain smooth segments and one dark red leaf-shaped tip; not a weapon carried in hand.
