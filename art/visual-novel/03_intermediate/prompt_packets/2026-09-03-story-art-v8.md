# Story art v8 prompt packet

## Global style lock

Japanese 2D anime cel-shaded visual novel art, classic Japanese shoujo Galgame aesthetic; clean simple linework, broad flat color shapes, restrained two-step cel shading, soft atmospheric depth, controlled highlights, low visual noise, gentle edges, cinematic but readable composition. No 3D render, no painterly photorealism, no western comic style, no mobile-game splash-art rendering, no over-detailed surfaces, no micro-textures, no film grain, no oversharpening, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## Standing sprite packet

- Edit from the supplied compact portrait for exact face, hair, species, palette, signature prop and silhouette identity.
- Use accepted full-body standing art only as scale, line, shading and transparency reference; do not copy another character's anatomy or clothes.
- Full body, three-quarter front, headwear through shoes visible, arms/props/tails inside canvas, clean native alpha, no floor shadow.
- Simplify embroidery, filigree, tiny chains, sparkle fields and material microtexture into large readable shapes.
- Intended runtime: 1024×1536 transparent WebP, visually legible when scaled to 80 percent stage height.

## Background packet

- Edit/extend from the accepted ash-registry family while preserving the same physical tower language: obsidian arches, copper registration hardware, blue-white index lights and violet seal fractures.
- 16:9 visual-novel background, open middle ground for two standing sprites, focal architecture above the dialogue box, no people.
- Lower 30 percent uses broad quiet shapes; no scattered paper confetti, illegible signage, tiny props, excessive bloom or sharp micro-contrast.
- Intended runtime: opaque 1672×941 WebP.

## Event CG packet

- Preserve supplied character references exactly: facial proportions, hair silhouette, eye colour, costume palette, signature accessories and height relationship.
- 16:9 cinematic visual-novel still, restrained two-step shading and large uncluttered shapes. The event must be understandable without on-image words.
- Leave the bottom 24 percent compositionally calm so the dialogue UI does not cover the essential action.
- Intended runtime: opaque 1672×941 WebP.

## Acceptance gates

1. Identity: no character, species, tail-count, hair, weapon or costume-colour drift.
2. Anatomy: two arms/two hands, coherent fingers, no merged prop or clipped extremity.
3. Style: flat cel shading, moderate contrast, no painterly grain or hyper-detailed ornament.
4. Composition: faces and evidence readable; lower UI-safe area does not contain the key story fact.
5. Technical: exact dimensions, alpha only on standing sprites, no baked checkerboard, no text/logo/watermark.
