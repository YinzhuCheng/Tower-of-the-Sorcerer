## Event CGs

### CG-10-F3-NORTHSTAR-ARRIVAL-V8

- Beat: after the tide core is released, Lanyin and Liyue finally hear the intact message that Northstar Seven and all forty-seven passengers reached the northern shore.
- Cast: Liyue left foreground, Lanyin right foreground, one translucent water-memory of the evacuation ship in the middle distance.
- Emotion: relief held inside exhaustion; evidence returning, not triumphal spectacle.
- Camera: medium-wide 16:9 from the archive floor, blue water light across both faces.
- Continuity: Liyue resolve identity, Lanyin lament identity, ocean archive architecture.
- Forbidden: readable text, extra people, modern ship, celebratory fireworks, noisy foam, photoreal water.

### CG-11-F17-SEVENTEEN-MINUTE-SPLICE-V8

- Beat: Lumi overlays the outage record and reveals that the sovereign command and the three local confirmations did not occur at the same moment.
- Cast: Liyue left, Lumi right, three luminous timeline ribbons and one displaced pulse between them.
- Emotion: quiet shock becoming precise understanding.
- Camera: medium-wide 16:9 in the star-mirror court; faces remain readable while the evidence occupies centre.
- Continuity: Liyue guarded/resolve identity, accepted Lumi focus identity, blue-violet star-mirror architecture.
- Forbidden: readable numbers or letters, holographic sci-fi UI, clocks, extra characters, noisy particle field.

## Coverage decision

Existing accepted CGs already cover the prologue, F10 truth, seal break, F19 ledger return, F20 accountability and F30 ending. Adding CGs at F3 and F17 closes the two largest explanatory gaps without turning routine mechanics exposition into redundant splash art.
