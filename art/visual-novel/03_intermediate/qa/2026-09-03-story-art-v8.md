- Stage-scale inspection at the runtime 80 percent actor size.

## Identity and style inspection

- Milu remains an anthropomorphic cat rather than a human catgirl; one tail, crescent shield and curved sword are intact.
- Feiye has exactly three tails and no pseudo-writing on her robe or seals.
- Lanyin retains deep-blue hair, shell harp and one simplified whale-shaped water spirit.
- Serena follows the frozen project canon: black-blue low ponytail, white/navy duelist uniform and one slender rapier.
- All accepted assets use broad cel-shaded shapes and controlled highlights; no film grain, painterly noise, watermark or readable text is present.

## Native alpha replacement and rejection record

The image generator baked its transparency preview grid into six earlier standing PNG attempts. The first Milu attempt was also too ornamented; the first Feiye attempt had four tails and pseudo-writing. Those opaque attempts remain under `04_cg/rejected/2026-09-03-story-art-v8/` as rejection history.

The final Milu, Feiye, Lanyin and Serena assets were replaced with the user's preferred 1024×1536 RGBA PNG masters from `00_source/story-art-v8-native-alpha/`. Each source already contains a native alpha channel, so no flood fill, color key or programmatic alpha recovery is applied. The PNGs and their WebP runtime derivatives were composited over a saturated green plate to inspect hair, white fabric, tails, weapons and thin edge lines; no checker squares, white halo or missing cutout regions remain.

## Automated gates

- Dialogue presentation tests require all five v8 stage mappings to report authored standing art.
- Story tests require the F3 and F17 CG paths and the new F22–F27 background mappings.
